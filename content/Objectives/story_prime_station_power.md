# Objective: [[Objectives/story_prime_station_power|Prime Station Power]]

## Narrative
> Lights, fabricators, and med kits all die when the generators starve. Stage fuel and charged cells so the night load does not brown out the whole town.

## Requirements
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/gasoline_canister|Gasoline Canister]]** (Deposit gasoline canisters)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/battery|Battery]]** (Deposit batteries)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/shock_capacitor|Shock Capacitor]]**

## Failure Penalty
Power margins collapse. Command shortens operating windows. (Defense: 0)
