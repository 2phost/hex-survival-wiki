# Objective: [[Objectives/story_daily_field_triage_drill|Field Triage Drill]]

## Narrative
> Medics report rising injury rates after recent relay interference. Stage stim packs and salvaged fabric at base—Command releases water and rations when quotas are met.

## Requirements
- Deposit 3x **[[Items/stim_pack|Stim Pack]]** (Deposit stim packs)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/salvaged_fabric|Salvaged Fabric]]** (Deposit salvaged fabric)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/research_material|Research Material]]**
- 1x **[[Items/bio_spike_pod|Bio Spike Pod]]**
- 2x **[[Items/water|Clean Water]]**
- 1x **[[Items/rations|Rations]]**

## Failure Penalty
Triage readiness slips and field confidence drops. (Defense: -1)
