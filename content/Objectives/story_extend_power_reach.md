# Objective: [[Objectives/story_extend_power_reach|Extend Power Reach]]

## Narrative
> Dead zones kill night work. Stage copper, relays, and panel stock so pole crews can push light farther from the core.

## Requirements
- Deposit 4x **[[Items/Unknown/copper_wiring|Copper Wiring]]** (Deposit copper wiring)
- Deposit 1x **[[Items/Unknown/damaged_solar_panel|Damaged Solar Panel]]** (Deposit damaged solar panel)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/Unknown/cracked_lens|Cracked Lens]]** (Deposit cracked lenses)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/Unknown/targeting_relay|Targeting Relay]]**

## Failure Penalty
Outer tiles stay dark and dangerous. (Defense: 0)
