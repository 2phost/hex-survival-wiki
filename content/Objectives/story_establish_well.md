# Objective: [[Objectives/story_establish_well|Establish the Well]]

## Narrative
> Command will not keep pouring rations into a dry camp. Dig and raise a working well so Outpost Alpha controls its own water instead of begging the uplink for every canteen.

## Requirements
- Build **well** (Build a Well)

## Rewards
- 6x **water**
- 1x **rations**

## Failure Penalty
Without a well, Command treats the outpost as non-self-sufficient. (Defense: -1)
