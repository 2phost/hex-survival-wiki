# Objective: [[Objectives/story_lock_down_artifacts|Lock Down Artifacts]]

## Narrative
> Unstable fragments are spiking fear and wrecking sleep. Contain reactor dust and hard flakes behind filter discipline before panic becomes policy.

## Requirements
- Deposit 1x **[[Items/reactor_dust|Reactor Dust]]** (Deposit reactor dust)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/obsidian_flake|Obsidian Flake]]** (Deposit obsidian flakes)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/filter_mesh|Filter Mesh]]** (Deposit filter mesh)

## Rewards
- 2x **[[Items/research_material|Research Material]]**
- 2x **[[Items/water|Clean Water]]**
- 1x **[[Items/bio_spike_pod|Bio Spike Pod]]**

## Failure Penalty
Containment fails. Morale and posture both slip. (Defense: -2)
