# Objective: [[Objectives/story_daily_ballistic_mesh_refit|Ballistic Mesh Refit]]

## Narrative
> Perimeter armor is thinning after the last wave. Deliver ballistic mesh and ceramic tiles for a hard refit.

## Requirements
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/ballistic_mesh|Ballistic Mesh]]** (Deposit ballistic mesh)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/ceramic_armor_tile|Ceramic Armor Tile]]** (Deposit ceramic armor tiles)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/alloy_plate|Alloy Plate]]**
- 2x **[[Items/scrap_metal|Scrap Metal]]**

## Failure Penalty
Soft sectors stay under-armored overnight. (Defense: -2)
