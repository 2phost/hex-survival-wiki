# Objective: [[Objectives/story_daily_solar_panel_reclaim|Solar Panel Reclaim]]

## Narrative
> Dust-scoured arrays are failing at noon peak. Stage damaged panels and spare copper so Engineering can restore cell output before the next brownout.

## Requirements
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/damaged_solar_panel|Damaged Solar Panel]]** (Deposit damaged solar panels)
- Deposit 3x **[[Items/copper_wiring|Copper Wiring]]** (Deposit copper wiring)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/solar_cell|Solar Cell]]**
- 1x **[[Items/battery|Battery]]**

## Failure Penalty
Daylight generation dips and night ops lose buffer. (Defense: -1)
