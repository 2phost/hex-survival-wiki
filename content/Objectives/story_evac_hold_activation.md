# Objective: [[Objectives/story_evac_hold_activation|Hold the Activation Line]]

## Narrative
> The beacon is live. Survive three consecutive midnights with base power above zero and town defenses intact while Command locks the convoy corridor.

## Requirements
- Hold activation for 3 midnights

## Rewards
None

## Failure Penalty
Activation stalled — the beacon reset and must be held again. (Defense: -4)
