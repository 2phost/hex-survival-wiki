# Objective: [[Objectives/story_phase2_choose_protocol|Select Victory Protocol]]

## Narrative
> The uplink displays two final directives. Your town must commit to Protocol Evac (extraction beacon) or Protocol Purge (seal corruption relays). Use the Command panel to choose before midnight.

## Requirements
- Commit to Protocol Evac or Protocol Purge

## Rewards
None

## Failure Penalty
Without a committed protocol, Command cannot route endgame assets. (Defense: 0)
