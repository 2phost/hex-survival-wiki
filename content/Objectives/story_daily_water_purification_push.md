# Objective: [[Objectives/story_daily_water_purification_push|Water Purification Push]]

## Narrative
> Dust storms contaminated reserve tanks. Command will release clean reserves once you deliver purification supplies.

## Requirements
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/Unknown/old_glass_bottle|Old Glass Bottle]]** (Deposit salvage bottles)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/Unknown/filter_mesh|Filter Mesh]]** (Deposit filter mesh)

## Rewards
- 5x **[[Items/Unknown/water|Clean Water]]**
- 1x **[[Items/Unknown/rations|Rations]]**
- 1x **[[Items/Unknown/resin_sealant|Resin Sealant]]**

## Failure Penalty
Purification kit incomplete; Command withholds clean reserves. (Defense: -1)
