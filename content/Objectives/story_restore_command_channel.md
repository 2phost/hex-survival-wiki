# Objective: [[Objectives/story_restore_command_channel|Restore the Command Channel]]

## Narrative
> The uplink is noisy and patrols are going silent. Recover radios, cells, and sensors so Command can hear Outpost Alpha clearly again.

## Requirements
- Deposit 1x **[[Items/broken_radio|Broken Radio]]** (Deposit broken radio)
- Deposit 2x **[[Items/battery|Battery]]** (Deposit batteries)
- Deposit 1x **[[Items/malfunctioning_sensor|Malfunctioning Sensor]]** (Deposit malfunctioning sensor)

## Rewards
- 1x **[[Items/research_material|Research Material]]**
- 1x **[[Items/targeting_relay|Targeting Relay]]**

## Failure Penalty
Dead air persists. Oversight tightens. (Defense: -1)
