# Vital: [[Vitals/health|Health]]

Health is a primary survival vital in Hex Survival. It represents your physical well-being. If it reaches 0, you die.

## 1. Taking Damage
Health is primarily lost through:
- **Monster Encounters**: Taking strike-back damage when attacking monster hordes (unless fully mitigated by combat consumables like [[Items/Unknown/mace_spray_common|Mace Spray]]).

## 2. Restoring Health
Health can only be restored through specific medical items. Some items may also provide minor hydration or AP alongside their healing properties.

| Item | Health Restoration | AP Restoration | Requirements |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **[[Items/Unknown/water_bottle|Bottle of Water]]** | **+10** | **+2** | None |
| **[[Items/Medical/field_bandage|Field Bandage]]** | **+20** | **0** | None |
| **[[Items/Medical/first_aid_kit|First Aid Kit]]** | **+40** | **0** | None |
| **[[Items/Medical/trauma_stabilizer|Trauma Stabilizer]]** | **+65** | **0** | None |
