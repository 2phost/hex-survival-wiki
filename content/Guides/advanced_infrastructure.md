# Guide: Advanced [[Base/index|Infrastructure]] & Grid Strategy

As your town survives the first few days, your focus must shift from simple survival to long-term sustainability and industrial dominance.

## 1. High-Tier Base Builds
Once the basic **[[Base/Constructions/watchtower|Watchtower]]** and **[[Base/Constructions/well|Well]]** are secure, prioritize these advanced structures:

- **[[Base/Constructions/research_lab_ii|Research Lab II]]**: A massive upgrade (120 Scrap) that expands the lab to four research slots and increases research speed by 20%. This is critical for scaling your town's technical capabilities.
- **[[Base/Constructions/battery_bank|Battery Storage]]**: Increases max power capacity by **+20%**. This provides a vital buffer for when the town is under siege or fuel is scarce.
- **[[Base/Constructions/fuel_refinery|Fuel Refinery]]**: Converts **[[Items/Unknown/chemical_sludge|Chemical Sludge]]** into stable generator fuel. This allows the town to survive even if **[[Items/Fuel/gasoline_canister|Gasoline Canisters]]** are not being found.
- **[[Base/Constructions/beacon_amplifier|Beacon Amplifier]]**: A specialized system that improves town-wide movement efficiency in the darkness.

## 2. Professional Grid Expansion
The "Loot Highway" is more than just a line of poles; it's the lifeblood of your town's industry.

### The Industrial Hub
To produce advanced materials like **[[Items/Unknown/alloy_plate|Alloy Plate]]s** and **[[Items/Unknown/logic_core|Logic Core]]s**, you must connect the grid to an **[[Biomes/industrial|Industrial Zone]]** or **[[Biomes/electronic_lab|Electronic Store - Lab]]** tile.
- **Step 1**: Build **[[Items/Power/power_pole|Power Poles]]** until they are adjacent to the target tile.
- **Step 2**: Ensure a player with **[[Skills/index|Field Engineering]]** (Lvl 1 for Industrial, Lvl 2 for Labs) is present to activate the facility.
- **Step 3**: Once powered, these tiles allow for the production of specialized components needed for the highest-tier builds.

### Base Defense
During the **Nightly Siege**, breaches are your greatest threat.
- **[[Base/Constructions/sentry_turret|Automated Sentry]]**: Rather than placing these on tiles, the Sentry is a centralized base upgrade that passively adds **+10 Town Defense** to protect against the Horde's attack strength at Midnight.

## 3. Power Grid Optimization
- **[[Base/Constructions/solar_panels|Solar Panels]]**: Every array you build reduces the daily base power drain by **10%**. In the late game, a large solar array can make your town almost self-sustaining during the day.
- **Grid Redundancy**: Avoid "single-pole" bottlenecks. If one pole is destroyed or a threat emerges nearby, a redundant "loop" in your grid can prevent an entire sector of the map from falling into darkness.
- **Scrap Workshop Buff**: When building your grid, consider the **[[Base/Constructions/scrap_workshop|Scrap Workshop]]**. It shortens the search cooldown by **25%** when searching at or next to the base—making it the perfect area for your most intensive scavenging operations.
