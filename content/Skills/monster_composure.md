# Skill: [[Skills/monster_composure|Monster Composure]]

![[assets/skills/monster_composure.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: +1 monster-tile fear resistance per level (max +5). When entering a hex with monsters, your party's combined resistance on that tile must meet or exceed the monster count to avoid gaining fear.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/field_medicine|Field Medicine]] Level 1
