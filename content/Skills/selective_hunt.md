# Skill: [[Skills/selective_hunt|Selective Hunt]]

![[assets/skills/selective_hunt.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Choose a light take that sometimes leaves local game in place. Highest ranks gently improve that hex's midnight recovery after light takes.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/field_dressing|Field Dressing]] Level 1




## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
