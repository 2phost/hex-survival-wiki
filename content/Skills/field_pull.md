# Skill: [[Skills/field_pull|Field Pull]]

![[assets/skills/field_pull.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Requires Escort Leadership 1. Pull a trusted friend from an adjacent tile onto your tile by spending your own movement AP. Each level grants 1 pull per day (max 5). The target must be your friend and have Trust enabled for you.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/escort_leadership|Escort Leadership]] Level 1

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 21 Days
