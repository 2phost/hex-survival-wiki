# Skill: [[Skills/careful_search|Careful Search]]

![[assets/skills/careful_search.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Optional slower searches that trade time for a better find chance. Activate Careful Search instead of a normal search.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/scavenger_eye|Scavenger Eye]] Level 1


## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
