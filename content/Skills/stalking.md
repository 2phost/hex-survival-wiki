# Skill: [[Skills/stalking|Stalking]]

![[assets/skills/stalking.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Spend 1 AP to prepare on a forest tile, then take a bonus to your next hunt there before midnight.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/track_signs|Track Signs]] Level 1




## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
