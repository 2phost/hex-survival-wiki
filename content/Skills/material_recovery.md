# Skill: [[Skills/material_recovery|Material Recovery]]

![[assets/skills/material_recovery.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: When dismantling fails to recover the structure itself, salvage a capped share of scrap materials instead.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/safe_dismantling|Safe Dismantling]] Level 1



## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
