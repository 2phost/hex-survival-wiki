# Skill: [[Skills/escort_leadership|Escort Leadership]]

![[assets/skills/escort_leadership.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Lead an escort group and invite friends to follow you. Each level allows 1 additional follower (max 5).
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/town_stewardship|Town Stewardship]] Level 1






## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 30 Days
