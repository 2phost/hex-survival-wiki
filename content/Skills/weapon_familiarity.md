# Skill: [[Skills/weapon_familiarity|Weapon Familiarity]]

![[assets/skills/weapon_familiarity.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Improve minimum weapon damage against monsters by +1 per level (up to +3). Does not change break or multi-kill chances.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/weapon_maintenance|Weapon Maintenance]] Level 1
