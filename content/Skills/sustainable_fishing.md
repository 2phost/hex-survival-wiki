# Skill: [[Skills/sustainable_fishing|Sustainable Fishing]]

![[assets/skills/sustainable_fishing.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Fish with less lasting depletion. Higher levels soften stock loss on catches and can boost a lake's midnight recovery after you fish it.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/water_reading|Water Reading]] Level 1
