# Skill: [[Skills/scavenger_eye|Scavenger's Eye]]
- **Description**: 5% higher chance to find loot when searching (per level). Does not auto-find; improves roll vs encounter.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Effect**: +5% higher success chance per search level.
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/preparedness|Preparedness]] Level 2

## Unlocks
- **At Level 1**: Enables [[Skills/salvage|Salvage]].
- **At Level 2**: Enables [[Skills/field_engineering|Field Engineering]].

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 30 Days
