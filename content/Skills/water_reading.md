# Skill: [[Skills/water_reading|Water Reading]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Preview adjacent lake fish stocks before casting. Higher levels also show your expected catch chance.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: None.







## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
