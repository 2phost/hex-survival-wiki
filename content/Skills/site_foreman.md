# Skill: [[Skills/site_foreman|Site Foreman]]

![[assets/skills/site_foreman.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Coordinate cooperative town builds. Pin a Priority Board of up to 3 builds, and when multiple distinct survivors recently donated materials, refund a small capped share of common materials on completion.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/construction_brief|Construction Brief]] Level 1



## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
