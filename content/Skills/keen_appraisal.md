# Skill: [[Skills/keen_appraisal|Keen Appraisal]]

![[assets/skills/keen_appraisal.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Preview likely loot families on your current tile before searching. Higher levels reveal more families. Does not improve find chance.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/scavenger_eye|Scavenger Eye]] Level 1



## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
