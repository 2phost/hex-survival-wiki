# Skill: [[Skills/drone_logistics|Drone Logistics]]

![[assets/skills/drone_logistics.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Tune cargo drone flight systems for faster deliveries. Level 1: 5 hour travel. Level 2: 4 hour travel. Level 3: 3 hour travel. Without this skill, cargo drones take 6 hours.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/drone_operator|Drone Operator]] Level 4





## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
