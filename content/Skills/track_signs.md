# Skill: [[Skills/track_signs|Track Signs]]

![[assets/skills/track_signs.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Read forest sign to improve hunting success. Anyone can hunt common game in forests; training raises the chance prey is taken before it flees.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: None.

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
