# Skill: [[Skills/brace|Brace]]

![[assets/skills/brace.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Spend 1 AP to brace for the next monster retaliation, reducing damage taken by 10% per level (up to 30%). Charge lasts until used.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/monster_composure|Monster Composure]] Level 1

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
