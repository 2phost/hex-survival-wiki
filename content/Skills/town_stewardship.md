# Skill: [[Skills/town_stewardship|Town Stewardship]]

![[assets/skills/town_stewardship.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Use the town resource tab responsibly. Level 0 lets you view and deposit resources only. Each trained level allows withdrawing 3 additional town items per hour.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Prerequisites**: None.

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 30 Days
