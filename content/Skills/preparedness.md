# Skill: [[Skills/preparedness|Preparedness]]
- **Description**: Start each new run with increasingly useful field gear. Lv 1: Starter Lamp. Lv 2: +1 Rations and +1 Water. Lv 3: +1 Stim Pack. Lv 4: +1 Makeshift Shiv. Lv 5: +1 Salvager Pack.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Effect**: Start with better gear based on your Preparedness level.
- **Prerequisites**: None.

## Unlocks
- **Level 1**: [[Items/Light/starter_lamp|Starter Lamp]].
- **Level 2**: Adds [[Items/Unknown/rations|Rations]] and [[Items/Unknown/water|Water]].
- **Level 3**: Adds [[Items/Unknown/stim_pack|Stim Pack]].
- **Level 4**: Adds [[Items/Weapon/makeshift_shiv|Makeshift Shiv]].
- **Level 5**: Adds [[Items/Unknown/salvager_pack|Salvager Pack]].




## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 30 Days
