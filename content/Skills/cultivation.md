# Skill: [[Skills/cultivation|Cultivation]]

![[assets/skills/cultivation.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Tend public farmland crops. Anyone can plant and harvest; training ripens crops sooner and can yield a bonus vegetable.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: None.


## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
