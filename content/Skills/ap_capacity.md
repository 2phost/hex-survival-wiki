# Skill: [[Skills/ap_capacity|AP Capacity]]
- **Description**: +1 max Action Points per level. Permanent character upgrade.
- **Max Level**: 5
- **Effect**: +1 max AP per level.
- **Prerequisites**: None.

## Unlocks
- **At Level 1**: Enables [[Skills/pack_mule|Pack Mule]], [[Skills/scavenger_eye|Scavenger's Eye]], [[Skills/stim_conditioning|Stim Conditioning]].
- **At Level 2**: Enables [[Skills/rest_recovery|Rest Recovery]].







## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
- **Level 4**: 14 Days
- **Level 5**: 30 Days
