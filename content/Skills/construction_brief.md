# Skill: [[Skills/construction_brief|Construction Brief]]

![[assets/skills/construction_brief.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Pin one unfinished town construction (or a thin stock work order) to every survivor's HUD so the crew can focus deposits. Open Construction and right-click a build to pin or unpin; Shift+right-click pins remaining materials as a work order.
- **Max Level**: 1
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/surveying|Surveying]] Level 1

## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
