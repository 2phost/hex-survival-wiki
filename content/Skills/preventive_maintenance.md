# Skill: [[Skills/preventive_maintenance|Preventive Maintenance]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Maintain field structures so repairs are cheaper and siege damage is reduced on poles you service.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/inspection|Inspection]] Level 1







## Training Times

- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
