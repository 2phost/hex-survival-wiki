# Skill: [[Skills/irrigation|Irrigation]]

![[assets/skills/irrigation.png|150]]

## Overview
- **Description**: Water crops more effectively so they ripen sooner. Anyone can water; training cuts more days off the wait.
- **Max Level**: 3
- **Prerequisites**: [[Skills/cultivation|Cultivation]] Level 1




## Training Times
- **Level 1**: 1 Hour
- **Level 2**: 24 Hours
- **Level 3**: 7 Days
